# The twelve expeditions — every stop, for artwork

One image per expedition (card banner, roughly 2.8:1) and one per leg (leg hero, portrait phone crop).
That is **12 banners + 64 leg images = 76 images**.

Distances and climbs are close to the real routes but rounded — worth checking against a guidebook before they are printed as fact in the app.

Grades: Gentle · Moderate · Hard · Severe. Opening three are the Camino, the Milford Track and the Grand Canyon — all three need their full leg sets. Nothing is completed yet, and the other nine stay hidden until they open.

Art in hand: Camino (banner + 6 legs), Milford (banner + 4 legs) and the Grand Canyon (banner + 4 legs) — all three open routes are fully illustrated. The remaining nine are drawn as they unlock.

## Notes for the artwork
- Banners read at about 362 × 126 in the card, so the subject wants to sit centre-left with sky above it.
- Leg images are used as full-bleed phone heroes behind text — keep the top third quiet, and avoid a bright band where the title sits.
- The three faith routes (Camino, Jesus Trail, Sinai) should feel like the same world as the rest, not a separate visual language.
- Time of day varies the mood, but the palette holds: warm ivory, sand, sage, charcoal, muted gold.
