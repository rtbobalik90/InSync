# InSync — Badge set

39 badges across 8 categories. Each stamp is a **category silhouette** plus a **per-badge device**. The silhouette says which category; the device says which badge. Draw the device only — the border is shared and already built in `Badges.dc.html`.

**Tiers:** Common (expected on the way through), Hard (requires sustained effort), Rare (few will hold it).

**Locked badges show nothing** — no name, no condition. These names are for you and the coach, not the UI.

## Drawing notes
- Devices sit in a **32×32 area centred at (50,50)** on a 100×100 viewBox.
- Stroke only, no fill. Weight 2.2–3.0. Round caps and joins.
- One colour, passed in — gold `#C6A15D` for Robert, sage `#8FA184` for Lizzie's own.
- Keep them legible at **78px**, which is the grid size in the category view.
- Sixteen of the thirty-nine devices are already built in `Badges.dc.html` under `MARKS`. The rest are listed above and not yet drawn.
